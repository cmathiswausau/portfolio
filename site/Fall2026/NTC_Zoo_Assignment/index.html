Endangered Species

Learn more about animals facing serious conservation challenges and the work being done to protect them.

Endangered

Tiger

Tigers are large cats native to Asia. Habitat loss and other human pressures have contributed to major declines in their historic range.

Tigers occupy forests, grasslands, and mangrove habitats. Conservation programs include protected areas, research, and managed breeding programs.

Source: Smithsonian's National Zoo

Critically Endangered

African Penguin

African penguins are coastal birds that spend much of their lives at sea and return to land to breed and raise chicks.

They are excellent swimmers and depend on healthy coastal and marine ecosystems. Conservation efforts focus on protecting breeding colonies and their food sources.

Source: Smithsonian's National Zoo

Endangered

African Elephant

African elephants are the largest living land animals and use their trunks for feeding, drinking, communication, and handling objects.

African elephants live in a range of habitats and face threats including habitat loss and illegal killing. Conservation work includes protecting habitat and supporting anti-poaching efforts.

Source: Smithsonian's National Zoo
